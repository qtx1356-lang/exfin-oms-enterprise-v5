# CODESTER SCREENSHOT GUIDE

Recommended 10-Screenshot Sequence for Codester Marketplace Listing:

1. **Screenshot 01 — Admin Portal Real-time Office Pulse**
   - Shows live map, attendance counters (Present, On Break, On Leave, Pending Exit).

2. **Screenshot 02 — Employee Mobile App Home Screen**
   - Highlights 25-meter Geofence Status badge, instant check-in status, and quick action bar.

3. **Screenshot 03 — Interactive Geofence Exit Checkout Confirmation Modal**
   - Demonstrates strict physical exit detection with actual timestamping.

4. **Screenshot 04 — AI Receipt OCR Expense Tracker**
   - Displays receipt photo upload and automatic Gemini AI extraction of vendor, total, and tax line items.

5. **Screenshot 05 — Work Planner & Task Kanban Board**
   - Shows interactive task status columns, deadline alerts, and employee productivity tags.

6. **Screenshot 06 — Algorithmic Efficiency & Analytics Dashboard**
   - Features efficiency scores, work hours graphs (`recharts`), and attendance statistics.

7. **Screenshot 07 — PDF Salary Payslip Generator**
   - Displays one-click printable PDF payslips with automatic tax deductions.

8. **Screenshot 08 — Admin Device Registration & Security Controls**
   - Shows hardware device fingerprinting, device approvals, and proxy attendance prevention.

9. **Screenshot 09 — Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Matrix**
   - Displays granular permission management for Super Admin, HR, Team Lead, and Employee roles.

10. **Screenshot 10 — Offline-First Startup & Sync Center**
    - Demonstrates instant offline application booting and dead-letter queue sync.
