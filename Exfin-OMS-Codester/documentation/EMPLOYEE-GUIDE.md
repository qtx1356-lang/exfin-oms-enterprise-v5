# Exfin OMS — Employee User Guide

This guide explains how employees use the Exfin OMS web and mobile application.

---

## 1. Initial Device Registration

1. Launch the Exfin OMS app on your smartphone or web browser.
2. Complete the registration form:
   - Full Name
   - Employee Code
   - Mobile Number & Email
   - Department & Job Designation
   - Capture live profile selfie photograph
3. Submit the registration. Your device ID is automatically bound to your account.
4. Wait for Admin/HR device approval notification before logging in.

---

## 2. Daily GPS Check-In & Attendance Modes

When arriving at work, grant location permissions when prompted. Select your attendance mode:

1. **Office Attendance**:
   - Must be physically inside the designated 25-meter office geofence boundary.
   - Click **Check-In**. System validates your distance from office center and records your precise GPS address.
2. **Work From Home (WFH)**:
   - Select WFH mode, enter mandatory task reason/plan, and click Check-In.
3. **Client Visit**:
   - Select Client Visit mode, enter client name and meeting purpose, and click Check-In.
4. **Outdoor Field Work**:
   - Select Outdoor Work mode, enter site location details, and click Check-In.

---

## 3. Office Exit Detection & Confirmation

When checking in at the office:
1. When you leave the office boundary (>25 meters distance), the system detects your geofence exit and records your exit time and coordinates.
2. Your status shifts to **Pending Exit Checkout**.
3. A confirmation dialog appears asking: *"You have moved outside the 25m office boundary. Would you like to confirm Checkout now?"*
4. Click **Confirm Checkout** to complete your day's checkout, or **Cancel / Return** if you are stepping out briefly and returning.

---

## 4. Expenses, Leave & Tasks

- **Submit Expense**: Click **Expenses > Add Claim**, fill in amount, category, description, upload receipt image, and submit.
- **Apply for Leave**: Click **Leave > Request Leave**, select leave type (Casual, Medical, Earned), date range, and reason.
- **Work Planner**: View assigned tasks, mark progress (`In Progress`, `Under Review`), upload proof of work, and submit for review.
